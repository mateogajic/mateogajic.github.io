CH.01 — Emission · Internship & BSc thesis @ NTC · Apr–Sep 2026

A photon is born in a bullseye

My bachelor’s thesis explored how Bragg structures can help photons escape a semiconductor — a design problem for single-photon sources at telecom wavelengths.

During my April–September 2026 internship at the Nanophotonics Technology Center (NTC, UPV), I designed and optimized circular Bragg (“bullseye”) structures and distributed Bragg reflectors (DBRs) for single-photon emission from GaSb/AlGaSb quantum dots in the telecom C-band. I defended the thesis in September 2026.

Using 3D FDTD simulations in RSoft FullWave, with sweep automation and post-processing in MATLAB, I improved the simulated extraction efficiency from 2% to 51.04% by including and optimizing a bullseye structure and a DBR, compared with a quantum dot in a bulk AlGaSb structure. This was a simulation result; the structure was not fabricated.

Thesis: Design and optimization of Bragg structures for single photon emission in GaSb/AlGaSb quantum dots in the telecom C-band.

CH.02 — The sweep · Research intern @ NTC · Oct 2024 – Jun 2025

You are the tunable laser

scroll to sweep λ across the C-band

During my October 2024–June 2025 research internship at NTC, I characterized optomechanical cavities using fiber and waveguide coupling, lasers, photodetectors, oscilloscopes and spectrum analyzers. I processed experimental signals in Python to extract resonance quality factors, optimized alignment and measurement procedures, and helped define fabrication ranges for reliable operation.

CH.03 — Acceleration · Innova Physics UPV · Sep 2023–May 2026

Designing an accelerator, together

At Innova Physics UPV, I led a team of more than 40 people working on the design and development of a particle accelerator prototype, bringing together applied physics, instrumentation, structures and technical validation.

From September 2023 to May 2026, I coordinated the team’s technical areas and presented our designs at a poster session at CERN, where we received feedback from CERN scientists on the design and experimental methods. I also contributed scientific posters to specialist conferences.
