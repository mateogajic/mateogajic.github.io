CH.01 — Emission · BSc thesis @ NTC · 2025 →

A photon is born in a bullseye

My thesis: design cavities that convince a quantum dot to send its photons where we want them — up and out, into a fiber.

At the Nanophotonics Technology Center (NTC, Valencia) I design circular Bragg (“bullseye”) gratings and distributed Bragg reflectors to increase photon extraction efficiency from GaSb/AlGaSb quantum dots emitting in the telecom C-band — the window where single-photon sources plug straight into existing fiber networks.

Full 3D FDTD simulation in RSoft FullWave; sweep automation and post-processing in MATLAB; geometry optimized against extraction efficiency and Purcell enhancement under realistic fabrication tolerances.
